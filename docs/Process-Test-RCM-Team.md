## Equipment breakdown before the worksheet (0351) — RCM-only tenants and templates

Until now a study read its components and parts live from the register on every load; a study on a manual tag, or on an asset with no children and no BOM, had nothing to pin to, name spares on, or draft through. Now the breakdown belongs to the study: `ers_rcm_study_items` (subunit / component / part, parent, critical, quantity, register links when it came from there, source register / bom / manual / template / specialist), `ers_rcm_failure_modes.study_item_id` as the pin target, and `ers_rcm_breakdown_templates` saved by ISO 14224 class and type (tenant scope, with a shipped-library scope reserved). A new tab "0 · Equipment" sits before the Worksheet: one table, add a row, paste a list ("TAG: Name *"), "Import from register" (merged by link, never duplicates), "Start from <template>" (best match by type, then class, own before library), "Save as template". The New Study form says what the study will start with; a study with nothing listed opens on Equipment. The Specialist prompts, text pinning, the worksheet's pin select, the Overview coverage card, the spares picker and the run-to-failure spares check all read the study's list through one adapter (`breakdownFromItems`), so an old study pinned by register id still counts. Items are frozen while approved; existing studies were backfilled from the register and their pins remapped. Duplicating a study copies its items and remaps the pins.

Verified (verify6): P-999 on a manual tag opened on Equipment, a pasted list of five became items with tag and critical flag, nesting held, "Save as template" stored five items with the parent link; the Specialist draft pinned 5 of 18 modes to the typed items with no register behind them and the Overview read "5 components · 0 BOM lines · 40% with a failure mode"; P-998 was offered "Start from Centrifugal pump — standard breakdown (5)", applied five items with source template and nesting, and applying twice added nothing; K-601's tab listed its three register components frozen while approved, and after Revise the worksheet's "Pin 4 rows to components" pinned 4 of 5 modes to the study items. Register hint on the New Study form: "Equipment breakdown: 3 components · 0 BOM lines from the register".

Phase 2 (0352): a shipped library of 22 ISO 14224 breakdowns (330 subunits and maintainable items, scope `library`, offered after a tenant's own template for the same class); "Suggest with the Specialist" on the Equipment tab proposes subunits and nested items from the asset context (source `specialist`); "Save N to register" turns typed items into child assets (tag `<parent>-<slug>`, level SUBUNIT/COMPONENT, parent's status) and parts into BOM lines and links the items and their modes; "Save as study template" on an approved study keeps breakdown, worksheet and decisions by class/type in `ers_rcm_study_templates`; "Apply to similar assets" on the RCM landing creates one draft per ticked asset of that class with no study (`rcm_source = template`, `derived_from_template_id`, `template_review_status = unreviewed`, its own operating context, register children merged in), and the approval guard refuses until the facilitator presses "Confirm review" on the Overview banner.
