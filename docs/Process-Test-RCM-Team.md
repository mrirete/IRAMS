- Audit trail: every write on the four RCM tables lands in `audit_logs` with the actor's email (admin, technician, supervisor and planner rows all present).

1. **Migration (mirror 0332/0333):** `ers_rcm_studies.collaborators jsonb`, `created_by uuid`; `rcm_caller_team_role`, `rcm_can_edit` (admin | reliability.edit | creator | team owner/editor), `rcm_can_approve` (admin | creator | team owner/reviewer); rebuild every `ers_rcm_*` policy on them (delete = admin); a BEFORE UPDATE trigger on studies: leaving `approved` needs `rcm_can_approve` and bumps `revision`; entering `approved` needs the completeness check in SQL and stamps the approver's uid.

5. **Point sheet:** prefill bands from `limitLibrary` by class; fix K-601's class and operating mode in the register.
