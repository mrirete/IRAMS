This notice explains what personal data IREAMS (Integrated Reliability and
Enterprise Asset Management System) processes, why, and what rights you have.
It is written for the two kinds of people whose data we touch:

- **Workspace users** — you use IREAMS because your employer (our customer)
  gave you an account. For this data your employer is the *controller* and
  Relantern is the *processor*: we handle it on their instructions. Rights
  requests go to your employer first; we support them (see §7).
- **Account owners** — you signed up for an IREAMS workspace yourself. For
  signup and account data Relantern is the *controller*.

---

## 1. What we collect

**At signup** (controller: Relantern): company name, your work email address,
and a password (stored only as a salted hash by our authentication provider).

**In your workspace** (controller: your employer): the people directory your
organisation maintains — name, work email, phone/mobile, job title,
department, work location/organisational unit, hourly rate where your
employer records it, work address, profile photo if you upload one, and
qualification/certificate records including scanned documents.

**Generated by using the product:** work orders, service requests, readings
and inspections you create or are assigned; hand-drawn sign-off signatures on
job safety analyses; messages and @mentions in record threads; notifications
addressed to you; an append-only audit trail recording who changed what and
when; invitation links sent to your email or phone.

**Technical:** authentication tokens (kept in your browser's local storage —
strictly necessary, not tracking), timestamps, and server logs at our
infrastructure providers.

**We do not** collect browsing history, advertising identifiers, or run
third-party analytics or tracking cookies.

## 3. Where your data lives

The production database, file storage, and authentication run on Supabase
hosted in **AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland)** — your data at rest stays in the EU.
The web application is served by Vercel's global edge network (the
application *code*, not your database contents). Email is dispatched via
Resend from `mail.relantern.com`. See the
[sub-processor register](subprocessor-register.md) for the full list.

## 4. AI features — what leaves the workspace

Some features send data to **Google's Gemini API** to generate analysis:
when you ask the assistant a question or run an AI analysis, the text of
your question plus relevant record context (e.g. work-order descriptions,
asset data) is transmitted to Google and the response returned. Free-text
fields may incidentally contain personal data (e.g. a technician named in a
work-order note).

- AI calls are made server-side through an authenticated proxy; your
  credentials are never shared with Google.
- AI usage is metered with a daily budget per workspace.
- We use the API under paid-tier terms where Google does not use submitted
  content to improve its products (Gemini API Additional Terms, verified
  2026-08-10) `[ACTION: confirm the production key's project is
  billing-enabled — this claim depends on it]`.
- If your organisation does not want record data reaching an AI provider,
  the AI features can be disabled per workspace on request.

## 5. How long we keep it

Per the [retention policy](data-retention-policy.md), in short: workspace
data is kept while your employer's contract runs and deleted or returned at
termination; account data while your account exists; audit trails are
retained as integrity records but the *person reference* in them is
pseudonymised when a person is erased; asset/maintenance engineering records
(which are about equipment, not people) are kept as long as the workspace
wants them.

## 6. Who can see it

Access is enforced in the database, per row: users see only their own
company's workspace (tenant isolation), and within it only what their role
permits. Storage objects (photos, documents, signatures) are private and
served only via short-lived signed URLs to authenticated members of the same
workspace. Relantern staff access production only for support and
operations, under the access-control policy. We do not sell personal data,
full stop.

  instruction within the timelines in the [DSR procedure](dsr-procedure.md).
- **Account owners:** email the privacy contact above. We respond within one
